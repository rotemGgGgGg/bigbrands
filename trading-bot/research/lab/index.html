Method

The same four gates for every idea.

Gate 1
Choose on 2012–19

Parameters are picked on the first eight years only.

Gate 2
Judge once on 2020–26

One look at the later years, no re-tuning after it.

Gate 3
Beat a random twin

Shifted clocks, shuffled days, random direction. p is the share of twins that did as well.

Gate 4
Survive Monte Carlo

Trades resampled into 5,000 years: chance of a losing year and the 95th-percentile drawdown.
