NQ/MNQ — 50 TradingView Pine Script Strategy Templates

The ZIP contains 50 separate .pine files plus this README.
All scripts are editable strategy templates for TradingView's Pine Editor.

Important:
- These strategies have NOT been backtested or independently validated here.
- No strategy is claimed to achieve a 70% win rate. The requested win rate is a research target, not a verified result.
- Win rate alone is not profitability. Evaluate net profit after fees/slippage, profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown, trade count, and out-of-sample results.
- Check each script for Pine Editor compilation issues and verify the signals on your chosen NQ/MNQ data feed and timeframe.
- Defaults use one contract, $2.50 commission per contract, and 2 ticks of slippage as placeholders; adjust to your broker/prop-firm setup.
- Some strategies depend on session settings or higher-timeframe data. Validate session timezone, bar size, data gaps, and futures rollover.
- Backtest first and paper trade before risking capital. Futures are leveraged and losses can be substantial.
